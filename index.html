About TriCure Healthcare

Quality treatment, accessible to all.

Doctors Pharmacists Hospitals Healthcare professionals Patients

TriCure Healthcare was founded in 2024 with a simple yet meaningful vision — to make quality healthcare more accessible and affordable to patients.

We began our journey with a single brand and a commitment to take one small step at a time. Since then, we have steadily expanded our portfolio by adding products designed to address evolving patient needs and meet the changing demands of the healthcare market.

Our journey began by serving a few selected areas across Mumbai. With consistent efforts, trust, and support from healthcare professionals and our partners, we gradually expanded our presence across the region.

We believe that quality treatment should not become a burden on patients because of its cost. Our aim is to provide quality medicines at a reasonable and responsible price, so patients can prioritize their health without hesitation.
